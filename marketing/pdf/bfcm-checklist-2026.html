Amazon Black Friday + Cyber Monday ads checklist 2026

Black Friday is Friday, November 27. Cyber Monday is November 30. Traffic and conversion rates rise, and CPCs rise with them. The sellers who win aren't the ones who spend the most; they're the ones who spend it on the right search terms.

Before Nov 17 to 25

  • Run a search term audit and add negatives for terms with clicks and no orders. Extra budget on junk terms just loses money faster.
  • Harvest converting search terms from auto and broad into exact campaigns, so winners have their own budget.
  • Check stock. No budget increases on SKUs with under 21 days of cover.
  • Confirm coupons and deals clear your breakeven after all fees (page 2).
  • Set peak-day budgets the night before, not on the morning of.

During Nov 26 to 30

Thu Nov 26Night beforePeak-day budgets set. Negatives in. Winners in their own exact campaigns.

The four checks, every peak day

  • Raise bids only on proven winners, max +20% per step. Go up in steps, check, then go again.
  • Check budgets. A campaign that's out of budget by noon misses the evening.
  • Tolerate ACoS up to breakeven on winners, on peak days only. Outside peak, hold your target.
  • Cut anything with 10 or more clicks today and no orders.

After Dec 1 to 5 what to turn back down

  • Pull bids back toward normal on Dec 1.
  • Stop tolerating ACoS up to breakeven: back to your target.
  • Check that Black Friday coupons have ended, or still clear breakeven at normal traffic.
  • Run a post-mortem audit on the peak week: what you spent, what sold, what leaked.
  • Keep winners funded through mid-December for gifting traffic.

Rocco Guardrails

Bid and budget guardrails

  • A proven winner is an exact-match term with orders and ACoS under target in the last 30 days. Only those get more.
  • Never raise a bid by more than 20% in one step.
  • New bid = current bid × (target ACoS ÷ actual ACoS), capped at 20% up or down.
  • If your target ACoS is at or above breakeven, fix the target before raising anything.
  • Don't touch a bid with too few clicks, or for a change under 5%.

Stock check

  • Days of cover for every advertised SKU, before you raise a budget.
  • Under 21 days of cover: don't surge that SKU.
  • Running out on Black Friday also hurts ranking after.
  • Out of stock or suppressed: fix the listing first, then the bid.

Coupon margin check

A coupon badge can lift click-through rate in search. But a 20% coupon usually cuts profit per unit by far more than 20%, because fixed costs don't shrink.

  • Shrinks with the discount: the referral fee and percentage-based coupon fees (charged on the discounted price).
  • Doesn't shrink: product cost, FBA fee, ad cost per unit, fixed coupon fees.
  • Find the deepest discount where profit per unit is still positive, with ad costs included.
  • Stay well above it in peak week, when CPCs rise.
  • Ask how many extra units the coupon must sell to earn what full price would. If that lift is unrealistic, go smaller.

Amazon's coupon fee structure has changed over time. Check the current fees in Seller Central before you run the numbers.

Breakeven, so you know where "too far" is

  • Breakeven ACoS = (price − referral fee − FBA fee − product cost − other costs) ÷ price. Above it, every ad sale loses money.
  • Target ACoS = breakeven ACoS − the profit margin you want to keep.
  • Each product has its own margin, so each has its own breakeven and target.
