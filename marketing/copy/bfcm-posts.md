### 2. the budget mistake

the most expensive black friday mistake on amazon isn't a bad bid.

it's a campaign that runs out of budget by noon. no budget, no evening sales.

set peak-day budgets the night before. then check them 3 or 4 times on the day.

the rest of the checklist is here: https://rocco.works/free/black-friday-checklist?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bfcm-checklist&utm_content=post-2

### 3. the coupon math

a 20% coupon does not cost you 20% of your profit. it usually costs a lot more.

the referral fee shrinks with the discount. your product cost, fba fee and ad cost per unit don't.

check the margin before the badge goes live. the checklist has the steps, and the calculator does the math: https://rocco.works/free/black-friday-checklist?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bfcm-checklist&utm_content=post-3

### 4. the 20% rule

raising every bid for black friday is how you pay more for the same junk clicks.

only raise bids on proven winners. max 20% per step. go up, check, go again.

and add your negatives first. extra budget on junk terms just loses money faster.

full checklist, free: https://rocco.works/free/black-friday-checklist?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bfcm-checklist&utm_content=post-4

### 5. dec 1 (post this on Dec 1)

black friday is done. now the part most sellers skip:

pull bids back toward normal.
stop tolerating acos up to breakeven. back to your target.
look at the peak week: what you spent, what sold, what leaked.
keep winners funded through mid december.

the "after" half of the checklist: https://rocco.works/free/black-friday-checklist?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=q4-checklist&utm_content=post-5

## Video hooks (for the batch system)

Each hook is the first scene of a video spec in `marketing/video/specs/`. Suggested scenes after the hook use existing templates. End card CTA: "link in bio" or "comment checklist".

### Hook A: the noon problem

- Hook lines: "Out of budget by noon." / "No evening sales."
- Highlight: "noon."
- Caption: the most expensive black friday mistake on amazon is a campaign that runs out of budget by noon.
- Then: face cam (shot A) "set budgets the night before, check them 3 or 4 times", then a screen recording (shot B) scrolling the PDF, then the end card.

### Hook B: the coupon

- Hook lines: "A 20% coupon costs" / "more than 20%."
- Highlight: "more"
- Caption: a 20 percent coupon usually cuts your profit per unit by a lot more than 20 percent.
- Then: screen recording (shot B) of the coupon calculator with made-up product numbers, labelled as an example on screen, then the end card.

### Hook C: before you raise a bid

- Hook lines: "Don't raise every bid" / "for Black Friday."
- Highlight: "every"
- Caption: don't raise every bid for black friday. only proven winners, 20 percent at a time.
- Then: desk clip (shot C) with captions listing the four peak-day checks, then the end card.
