## Creating the channel as a brand account (under Praveen's Google login)

YouTube changes its menus often: if a step is not where this says, look for the same words nearby.

1. Sign in to YouTube with your own Google account.
2. Click your picture (top right) → Settings → Account.
3. Under "Your channel", click "Add or manage your channel(s)".
4. Click "Create a channel". Type the channel name (option 1 above). This creates a Brand Account, which is what lets you add managers later without sharing your Google password.
5. Tick the box to agree, then Create. You are now acting as the new channel (check the picture, top right).
6. Go to YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info. Set the handle (first free one from the list), paste the description, add the links in order.
7. Customization → Branding. Upload `watermark-150x150.png` as the picture, `banner-2560x1440.png` as the banner, and `watermark-150x150.png` again as the video watermark (display time: entire video).
8. Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility. Verify with your phone number: needed for custom thumbnails and videos over 15 minutes.
9. Create the five playlists (Content → Playlists → New playlist), public, with the titles and descriptions above.
10. Upload the trailer. Customization → Layout → "Channel trailer for people who haven't subscribed": pick it.
11. Settings → Permissions: nobody to add yet. This is where a helper would go later, as Editor, never Owner.
12. Put the handle into `marketing/config/offer.ts` (the YouTube handle placeholder) so every asset picks it up.
