Rocco is an AI Amazon manager. You approve, Rocco does the rest.

Someone has to check ads, bids and stock every morning. Rocco does that check on your Seller Central and Amazon Ads accounts and turns it into a few clear jobs, ranked by money:

• Block searches that waste money
• Fix bids that overspend (capped, explained, undoable)
• Turn winners into exact campaigns
• Move budget to what's profitable
• Stay in stock: restock warnings, and ads eased on products that can't ship
• Tidy campaigns: one naming system, one portfolio per product
