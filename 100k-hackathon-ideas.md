## 2. Ideas discussed so far (with verdicts)

### A. Prize-share referral scheme (original idea)
Promise 50% of the prize to the contacts and followers who help.
- **Verdict: ❌ Drop.** Friends paying because they were promised a cut looks like recycled revenue. It's a legal and lottery-style risk, and mass DMs get accounts restricted.
- **Keep the spirit as an affiliate army:** 30–50% commission on real sales, with a public leaderboard.

### B. Tools for Amazon / FBA sellers (agency work turned into software)
Turn a repetitive agency task (listing audits, PPC checks, reimbursements) into software.
- Pros: warm network, word-of-mouth trust, business pricing.
- Cons: not viral, and cold outreach is slow in 30 days because a new domain can't be warmed up for email in time.
- **Verdict: 🟡 Safe, low-variance fallback.**

### C. Wrapped / year-in-review engine (multi-audience)
One engine with a separate module per audience: AI Wrapped (ChatGPT/Claude exports), Netflix, Instagram, WhatsApp/Couple (gift), Founder/Business (Stripe CSV), UPI money, Swiggy/Zomato, Amazon/Flipkart, LinkedIn, X.
- Market: Spotify Wrapped 2025 had 300M+ engaged users. The third-party paid Wrapped market is roughly $1–5M worldwide (low-confidence estimate).
- Learning: the "2025 Wrapped" app hit #1 Lifestyle on the App Store but was free with no in-app purchases. Popularity doesn't equal profit.
- Risks: crowded (many WhatsApp Wrapped apps), Spotify owns the "WRAPPED" US trademark (don't use it as a brand or domain), and another entrant is planning it.
- **Verdict: 🟡 Parked.** The architecture still works (parse files in the browser, Polar/Razorpay checkout, webhook unlock).

### D. "All AIs in one app"
- **Verdict: ❌ Too crowded** (CosmicUp, ChatBuddy, ChatXOS, Merlin, MultipleChat and others).
- Spin-off kept: **"One brain" extension** that stores your personal context once and injects it into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini.

### E. Launch Grade scanner
Paste a URL and get an A–F grade for **AI visibility** (do ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity recommend you?), **security** (exposed keys, open databases), and speed/SEO/llms.txt. Free grade with a share card, $19–29 fix report including copy-paste fix prompts for Cursor/Lovable/Claude Code, $19–49/month monitoring.
- Evidence: scans found 98% of 1,072 vibe-coded apps had at least one flaw. Paid AI visibility tools cost $65–399/month.
- Viral hook: a public "Hackathon Launch Grades" leaderboard for entrants.
- Risks: each half is crowded (VibeSafe, VibeWrench, Zarla, Maverank). Only scan sites with the owner's consent.
- **Verdict: 🟢 Strong builder/business play.**

### F. Build-in-public autopilot
Turns GitHub commits and Stripe numbers into daily X, LinkedIn and Instagram posts. Every hackathon entrant needs to post daily.
- **Verdict: 🟢 Good fit for entrants.** Small market but easy to spread.

### I. Live 30-day "build with me" cohort (₹3,000, daily 1-hour videos, WhatsApp group)

### J. Simple game living on ad revenue
- **Verdict: ❌ for the contest.** Ad revenue probably can't be verified on TrustMRR, the money per view is tiny, and app store review plus Google Play's closed-testing period eat into the month.

## 3. New directions (researched Nov 16, after the rules dropped)

The biggest insight from the rules: **AI costs don't reduce your score, and Razorpay counts.** That makes paid AI generation for Indian buyers a real option.

### K. 🟢 Desi AI Trend Studio (top pick for "unique and viral")
A web app where Indian users put **their own photo** into whatever AI photo or video trend is going viral **that day**, and pay ₹49–99 per video via UPI.
- Why now: trends like **"Sheru Bhai" (an AI lion boss)** are spiking in India (Google Trends interest roughly 5x between Nov 6 and Nov 11). Global trend apps (makememe.video, makeviral.ai, Trendsurf) are US-focused and charge in dollars.
- **Edge:** India-first trends, Hinglish, UPI, WhatsApp-native sharing, and **a new trend added within 24 hours**. Speed is the moat.
- Viral loop: the output *is* the reel or WhatsApp status, with a small watermark plus link.
- Seasonal add-ons on the same engine: **Shaadi trailer** (₹499–1,999 from couple photos), Christmas/New Year greeting videos, pet trend videos.
- Score maths: AI generation costs aren't deducted, so ₹99 minus about 2% fees is almost all profit. 2,000 videos a day for 20 days ≈ ₹40 lakh revenue (best case). 200 a day ≈ ₹4 lakh (realistic floor if one trend takes off).
- Must-dos: label content as AI-generated (India's IT Rules require labels on realistic synthetic media), accept only the user's own photo with a consent checkbox, no public figures or minors, avoid religious and deity imagery.
- Risks: model quality and cost per video, latency, a crowded global category, and trends dying fast.

### L. 🟡 Hackathon launch directory + live board
Explicitly allowed ("launch directories, paid listings"). Thousands of entrants need visibility; sell $19–49 featured spots and badges.
- Fast to build, spreads on its own among entrants, but has a small ceiling (~$2–10K) and Marc's own boards (TrustMRR/Unicorne) compete.

### M. 🟡 AI "rate my ___" score cards
"AI rates your dating profile / LinkedIn / Instagram grid / resume" with a shareable score. Cheap to build, built to be shared, and AI costs don't count. Charge for the full report and rewrite.

### N. 🟡 "No ads, no subscription" daily-tools suite (discussed Nov 17)
Many everyday consumer tools in one place, with no ads and no subscription.
- **Fully free = $0 profit = can't win** a profit-scored contest. Only works as **pay-once** (lifetime unlock), which counts in full.
- Demand signal: subscription fatigue is a strong 2026 trend; 41% of consumers report it; lifetime options are returning; "pay once, own forever" indie apps are coming back.
- Problems: generic utility tools are commoditized (free ad-supported sites + ChatGPT/Claude do most of it); low price (~$9–29 / ₹199–499) needs thousands of buyers; SEO for hundreds of tool pages takes months; paid apps get less discovery.
- Only sharp versions: (a) a focused **pay-once alternative to one specific hated subscription** with a big user base, or (b) free tools used as an **acquisition engine** for a paid product (a GTM tactic, not a product).
- **Verdict:** 🟡 good positioning ("pay once, no ads"), weak as a broad product for 29 days.

### O. 🟡 "Phone app audit": suggest cheaper / pay-once alternatives to your apps (discussed Nov 17)
User screenshots their home screen + App Store/Play subscription list (or uploads a bank/UPI statement) → AI lists every app/subscription, yearly cost, and free / pay-once / cheaper alternatives + cancel steps + a shareable "your phone costs ₹X/year" card.
- **Technical reality:** iOS apps can't read your installed-app list; Android restricts it under Play policy. Input must be screenshots or statements.
- **Already exists (partially):** Subscription Track: AI Insight (AI suggests cheaper alternatives), AI Subscription Trap Scanner, Rocket Money (bank-linked + cancellation service), Bobby, Finny, Tilla (trackers, some pay-once); plus "one-time-purchase alternatives" listicles and AlternativeTo.
- **Money problem:** you're recommending other people's apps. Affiliate commissions don't count on TrustMRR; only a ₹99–199 / $5–9 report fee does, so thousands of buyers are needed.
- **Possible edge:** India-first (UPI AutoPay mandates, Indian apps/OTT bundles), screenshot-in → instant shareable savings card (viral), no bank login needed.
- **Verdict:** 🟡 fun, shareable, privacy-friendly, but crowded-ish and low-ticket for a profit contest.

## 4. Recommendation (as of Nov 16)

1. **Main bet:** K, Desi AI Trend Studio. It's unique for India, the output itself spreads, Razorpay counts, and AI costs don't hurt the score.
2. **Backup / B2B hedge:** E, Launch Grade, sold with annual plans (which count in full).
3. Distribution for either: Instagram reels of real outputs, the affiliate army (commissions), the WhatsApp network, and building in public with daily numbers. **Avoid paid ads** unless a reel is already working, since ad spend comes off profit.
