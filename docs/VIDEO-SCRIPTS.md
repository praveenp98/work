# Rocco marketing video scripts (Nov 25, 2026)

Built from what sellers say **at home**, not in PPC forums: money, control, "is my agency/AI actually doing a good job?", "can I take a day off?". One idea per video. Everything shown on screen must be real Rocco screens (the example account is labelled). Never quote Reddit posts or other people's numbers as facts. Never say "never changes without asking": say "nothing changes until you say so" (autopilot is opt-in).

Format for all: vertical 9:16 for Reels, Shorts, TikTok and X; 16:9 cut for the site and YouTube. Burned-in captions, because 85% watch muted. You on camera for the hook, then the screen.

**The core line (use everywhere): "Claude gives advice. Rocco keeps the receipts."**

---

## 1. "We did $80k last month. How much did we make?" (30s) — the spouse conversation
| Time | You say | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "My wife asked how much we made last month. I said eighty grand. She said no, how much did we *make*?" | Your face, kitchen or desk, natural light |
| 3–10s | "That's the Amazon seller problem. Revenue's easy. Amazon takes fees, ads take the rest, and you don't know where it leaks." | Quick cut: Seller Central numbers (blurred) |

| 22–30s | "And a week later it tells me if the fix actually made money. $279 a month, flat. rocco.works" | Change log "worked · $212/mo" → end card |

## 2. "Bro, just give Claude your Excel files." (35s) — the sibling conversation (the Claude angle)

## 4. "Can you take a vacation?" (20s) — the parents conversation
- 0–4s: "My mom asked if I can ever take a vacation. Honestly? Not really. Amazon doesn't sleep."
- 4–14s: Rocco's office in night mode → morning glow → "3 jobs waiting for your OK" → you approve from WhatsApp on a beach chair (staged, your phone).
- 14–20s: "Now Rocco does the morning rounds and texts me. I reply YES. rocco.works"

## 5. "The 12-second hook" (Reels and Shorts)
"My Amazon manager answers texts." → phone: "why did sales drop yesterday?" → Rocco's answer with numbers → "YES G1" → "Done in Amazon ✅" → "rocco.works".

## 6. Founder story (60–90s, X and LinkedIn)
1. "I run an Amazon brand. I built Rocco because I was the guy checking ads at midnight."
2. Show the strategy step: "I tell it profit or growth, my budget limit, what never to cut."
3. Show the morning: jobs ranked by money, approve, done in Amazon.
4. Show the receipts: change log, result a week later, undo.
5. "Dr. PPC charges $299 plus 2% of your ad spend. Rocco is $279 flat. Two weeks to try it on your own account, every cent back if it's not worth it."
