## Play A: "Shaadi Party Kit" (India) ⭐
One ₹2,999–4,999 kit, made in 15 minutes from a short form about the couple:
1. **Live sangeet game** on a projector: guests scan a QR and play "How well do you know Rohan & Priya?" with AI-written questions, photo rounds, Team Bride vs Team Groom, AI roast commentary
2. **Personalized sangeet/couple song** with their names and story (Hinglish, Punjabi, etc.)
3. **Cinematic invite / thank-you video** for WhatsApp

**Why explosive:** each kit puts the brand in front of **200–500 guests three times** (QR on the big screen, the song at the sangeet, the forwarded video), many of whom have weddings coming. Each piece exists separately; **nobody bundles all three, AI-personalized, for Indian weddings**.
**Deadline:** sangeets for Nov 21–Dec 12 weddings are happening now; Jan–Mar 2027 couples prepare in December.
**Buyer:** the sibling/cousin/friend organizing the sangeet (impulse + love + budget).
**Risks:** three products to make reliable at once; pickiness and refunds; music licensing (use a commercially licensed music API); the Feb 2026 AI-label rules apply to video (label + metadata).
**Odds:** P($15K+) ~12% · P(#1) ~3%

## Play B: "Hackathon Launchpad + Throne" (the contest-native stunt)
A launch directory for the 3,500+ hackathon entrants (directories and paid listings are **explicitly allowed**):
- Free listing for every entrant (they need traffic and customers; the official leaderboard shows revenue, not discovery)
- Paid boosts ($19–49) and a daily "Top 5 to watch" X thread / newsletter
- **The Throne:** one top spot, price = last price + $1, every takeover auto-posted on X tagging the new and old king. Revenue grows fast: 250 takeovers ≈ $31K (1+2+…+250)
**Why explosive:** a captive, competitive audience that craves visibility; a meta story Marc/Jack may amplify; built in 1–2 days.
**Risks:** mechanic is cloned elsewhere (fatigue); entrants are frugal; manual review could see it as "not a real business"; organizers may not like riding their brand (use "unofficial", ask them).
**Odds:** P($15K+) ~9% · P(#1) ~2%
