# Competitor map (researched Nov 17, 2026)

Prices are published starting prices from 2026 comparison articles; verify on vendor sites before quoting them in marketing.

> **Naming note:** Amazon renamed Rufus to **Alexa for Shopping** on May 13, 2026. Sellers and the ads console still say "Rufus" and "Sponsored Prompts." Use both terms in SEO pages ("Rufus / Alexa for Shopping").

**MCP / AI-agent layer (new in 2026):** Amazon's official Ads MCP Server (open beta since Feb 2, 2026), plus third-party servers: WisePPC MCP, Adbrew MCP, Trellis agents, MCP Playground "Amazon Ads Agent." Several let Claude/ChatGPT run ad audits by chat.

**Takeaway:** PPC automation is crowded and priced at $78–700+/mo, often with a % of spend. **Don't compete as another bid optimizer.** Compete on: instant audits with no setup, a Sponsored Prompts (Rufus ads) audit, an upload-first workflow, flat annual pricing with no % of spend, and an approval-first AI copilot.

## 2. Rufus / Alexa for Shopping listing optimization (Pillar B)
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Azoma | Rufus visibility at brand/category/ASIN level + AI listing optimization (positions itself as best overall) |
| Ecomtent | Shopper questions → listing content; AI images/A+; Amazon AI Scorecard vs the COSMO knowledge graph |
| SalesDuo AI Creative Studio | Images, A+ drafts, titles, bullets, description (launched Sept 2026) |
| Dageno AI | Cross-channel AI shopping visibility |
| Rufusly | Rufus optimization guides/tools |
| Helium 10 (Listing AI) | Rufus-ready listing rewrites inside the suite |

**Takeaway:** Rufus listing tools exist but are new and mostly listing-only. Our edge: connect **listing gaps → Sponsored Prompts spend** (the ads side) in one report.

## 3. Sponsored Prompts (Rufus ads) management
- No dedicated standalone tool found. Coverage is mostly **blog/education content** from PPC tools (Adbrew, Ad Badger, Intentwise, Acadia, Trellis).
- Amazon offers a Prompts tab per ad group and a Prompt report, with options to enable or disable each prompt.
- **Takeaway: the most open gap → lead product angle.**

## 4. Appeal / Plan of Action tools (Pillar C)
| Player | Price |
|---|---|
| Human consultants / law firms | $1,500–3,000+ per appeal |
| AI POA tools (e.g. PlatformAppeal €29.99/mo, SOT Shield $249 pro tier, appeal.tools) | €30/mo to $249 |
| Free Claude/agent "skills" for suspension appeals | Free |

**Takeaway:** commoditizing. Keep it as a $79 urgent add-on and a lead magnet, not the core. Must stress verifiable facts (AI drafts that invent root causes get rejected).

## 5. All-in-one seller suites (indirect)
- **Helium 10:** free plan; Platinum $129/mo ($99 annual); Diamond $359/mo; Starter retired in April 2026's price increase
- **Jungle Scout (Catalyst):** from ~$29/mo annual, no free plan, 7-day money-back
- **Also:** SellerSprite, ZonGuru, SellerApp, SmartScout, AMZScout, Viral Launch
- **Profit tools:** Sellerboard, Shopkeeper, others. **Reimbursement services:** GETIDA, Seller Investigators, Refunds Manager (usually take a % of recovered money)

## 6. Agencies (the "status quo" we replace)
PPC-only $5K–15K/mo; full-service $8K–25K/mo, or 5–15% of revenue. A reported 67% of agency clients are dissatisfied with the strategic guidance.
