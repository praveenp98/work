# If Claude had to win: what it would build (Nov 17, 2026, 02:05 IST)

**Answer: the boring one. A single Amazon ad-waste + Rufus-ads audit tool, sold as capped "Founding Lifetime" seats to people who already trust Praveen.**

## Why this beats the viral plays
- **The contest is won by profit, not attention.** One $799 lifetime seat = ~23 Shaadi kits at ₹2,999 = ~800 trend videos at ₹99.
- **Winning likely needs $15–60K.** That's ~50 buyers at ~$800, or about 3,000 consumer purchases. Fifty is reachable through warm relationships; 3,000 needs luck.
- **It uses Praveen's only real unfair advantage:** years of Amazon account work and a network of brand owners and agency people. A viral consumer product starts from zero like everyone else.
- **The product doesn't need to beat Helium 10 or DataDoe.** Warm buyers buy from the person they trust, if the tool saves them real money on day 1.

## What to build (and nothing more)
1. **Upload search-term report → in 60 seconds:** wasted $ in the last 30 days, negatives, harvest list, bid changes (±20% cap), **upload-ready bulk sheet**
2. **Sponsored Prompts (Rufus ads) audit** from the prompts report: what you're auto-paying for, what to pause
3. **Weekly email reminder** to upload again → "your waste went from $X to $Y"
No connector, no API, no 25 tools. A clean web app, Razorpay + Polar → TrustMRR.

## Offer
- **Founding Lifetime:** $299 (single brand) · **$799 (agency/up to 15 brands)**, capped at 100 seats total
- Free: wasted-$ total for one report (the hook)
- 7-day refund

## How it gets to ~50 buyers
- **Days 1–3:** list every Amazon person Praveen knows (brand owners, agency owners, freelancers, ex-colleagues, community friends). Personal message: "I built the tool I use internally. Upload one report, it shows your wasted spend in 60 seconds. First 100 get lifetime." Target 150 personal messages.
- **Daily LinkedIn:** real anonymized waste numbers ("this brand wasted $2,140 last month on 38 terms") + Audit Friday (comment AUDIT).
- **Agencies are the multiplier:** one agency owner = $799 and uses it on many brands, and they talk to other agencies.
- **Amazon seller communities:** value-first posts with free audits.

## Guardrails (eligibility)
- New brand and domain; never the ShelfPilot/FBAmz name.
- Self-serve only: no calls, no "we'll manage it."
- **Don't route existing agency clients' money into it.** Sell to *new* relationships first; if an existing client buys, it must be a normal checkout for the tool, not part of agency fees.
- Keep a clean record of who bought and how they found it (for manual review).

## Honest odds
P($15K+) ~12–15% *if* Praveen can name 30+ Amazon people who'd reply to him personally. Without that network, the Shaadi Party Kit has better upside.
