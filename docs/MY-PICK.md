### The hidden edge in the rules
Contest profit = revenue − refunds − fees − **ad spend**. **AI/API costs don't count.**
- A normal AI gift company needs ROAS ~2–3 to survive (it pays for generation, staff, etc.).
- **For the contest score, any ROAS above ~1.1 adds profit.** Example: $40K ad spend at ROAS 1.6 → $64K revenue → ~$22K contest profit.
- So Praveen can **bid harder than established competitors** in a proven category during these 30 days, and win the auction they can't afford.
- Real cash still matters: ad spend is paid upfront; check payout timing (Razorpay settles in ~2 days; check Polar's payout schedule) and set a hard daily loss limit.
