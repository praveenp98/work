# Kill test: Homebuyer Case Builder (Nov 17, 2026, 02:45 IST)

## How big is it? (official complaint data)
- UP RERA: 58,545 complaints since inception = **39% of all RERA complaints in India** → national total ≈ **1.5 lakh complaints in ~8 years** (≈ 18–20K/year).
- New filings per year: MahaRERA ~5,000 (5,073 in 2025); UP RERA 2,394 in 2025; Karnataka 12,772 cumulative.
- So roughly **1,500–2,000 RERA complaints are filed nationwide per month.** Consumer-commission real-estate cases add some, but the same order of magnitude.

## Contest math
- $15K ≈ ₹12.5L ≈ **250 buyers at ₹4,999**, or 125 at ₹9,999, inside 29 days.
- That is **~12–15% of everyone filing in India this month**, for a brand-new, unknown brand. Realistic capture for a new tool: 1–3% → ₹1–3L (~$1.5–3.5K).
- Association plan (₹40–50K) would need 25–30 associations to close in a month; associations decide slowly and usually already have a lawyer.

## Other killers
1. **Stakes push people to lawyers.** With ₹50L–2Cr at stake, most buyers pay ₹15–25K for a lawyer rather than trust new software.
2. **Once-in-a-lifetime purchase**: no repeat, no word-of-mouth loop inside the contest window.
3. **The form is simple.** RERA complaints are filed online in a standard form; the hard part is hearings, which software can't attend.
4. **Legal-advice and quality risk**: a weak draft hurts a real case; disclaimers reduce trust.
5. **Search volume is modest** and SEO won't rank in 29 days; paid search on legal keywords is expensive for ₹10–20K.

## Verdict
**KILL for the contest.** Real problem, but the market is ~20K filers/year, so it can't produce 250 sales in 29 days. Could be a slow, real side business later (SEO + per-project landing pages), not a $15K-in-a-month play.
