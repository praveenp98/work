# GTM playbook: every angle, 30 days

**Target:** ~250 paying buyers × ~$199 average ≈ **$50K** collected by Dec 16.
Contest maths: one-time, annual and lifetime payments all count as money received (annual/lifetime count in full). Fewer high-ticket buyers can replace volume: 25 Brand annual ($1,499) ≈ $37K.

**Funnel reality:** at ~3% visitor → paid, 250 buyers needs ~8,000 engaged visitors (people who upload/paste). At ~0.4% sent → paid, cold email alone would need ~60,000 sends, but warmup caps us at ~5,000–6,000. **No single channel gets there. Stack all of them.**

## 2. Offers that raise conversion
- **Free 60-second audit** (Ad Waste + Rufus Ads) is the front door everywhere
- **Black Friday / Cyber Monday (Nov 27 – Dec 1):** "Founding Member" offer, e.g. Pro annual at $199 (instead of $299) or **Founding Lifetime $499, capped at 100 seats.** Lifetime counts in full for the contest; the cap protects long-term revenue
- **Agency bundle:** first 10 agencies get 2 extra workspaces free
- **14-day refund guarantee**, stated loudly
- **Urgency that's true:** "Sponsored Prompts are already billing you" and "Q4 peak ad waste"

## 4. Cold email specifics
- Main domain never sends cold. 2–3 sending domains × 3 inboxes, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, warmup from Nov 16–17
- Verify the whole list; bounces under 2%, complaints under 0.1%; one-click unsubscribe; physical address; skip EU/UK unless the GDPR basis is clear
- Segment: (a) brands running Sponsored Products (Rufus angle), (b) brands with an agency ("67% of agency clients are unhappy" angle), (c) agencies (white-label angle)
- 3-step sequence: hook + free audit link → short proof number → "want the bulk sheet?"
- Kill rule: under 1% replies after 1,000 sends → change segment/copy before sending more

## 6. Measurement
- UTM on every link (channel, campaign, post)
- Weekly: buyers and revenue by channel, cost by channel, cost per buyer
- Double down every Monday on the top 2 channels; cut anything with zero buyers after 10 days

## 7. Guardrails
- Never mass-DM on Instagram/LinkedIn (accounts get restricted); keep it personal and capped
- No fake reviews, testimonials or income claims; only real anonymized numbers with permission
- Log every marketing cost (DB, ads, sponsorships, commissions, Apollo credits) for the profit calculation
