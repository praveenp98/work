# Jobs AI can automate: are they easy to reach, convert and market? (Nov 17, 2026)

Brief from Praveen: check the current job market, find jobs AI can easily automate, then check whether buyers for those are easy to reach, convert and market to.

## 1. What the job market says (2026 data)
- Postings for the most AI-exposed jobs fell most between May 2022 and May 2026 (Indeed Hiring Lab). Revelio, the Dallas Fed and Goldman report the same, with the hit concentrated on **junior** roles.
- But over the last 12 months the pattern flipped: more exposed jobs rebounded more, and US software developer postings are **up ~15%** since Claude Code launched (Indeed). Coders are not the target.
- Hardest hit: **customer service reps** (down 130K, −4.8%, May 2024 → May 2025, per BLS), data/document clerks, admin/scheduling, recruiting.
- Small businesses (the realistic buyer): AI's top uses are **customer service, marketing, bookkeeping, sales** (PYMNTS, Oct 2026). Skipping a hire is ~4x more common than a layoff. 75% plan to give at least one function to software/AI instead of hiring in the next 12 months (Bluevine). 86% of solopreneurs try AI before hiring (FreshBooks).
- India (Apna / WorkIndia): the most-posted roles are **telecaller, data entry operator, back office, receptionist, accounts assistant**, paid ₹10–20K/month.

**Honest conclusion:** the jobs that are *most* automatable are exactly where hundreds of "AI employee" startups already compete, often funded and often at $29–99/month. Selling to small businesses is also slow: you need trust, integrations and setup, and the contest has 29 days. **No "replace job X" category scores well on reach, conversion and marketing at once.**

## 3. The useful insight: job posts are buying signals
A business that just posted a job for a telecaller, receptionist or data-entry role has **budget, pain and urgency right now**. Two ways to use that:
1. **Free hook tool, "Should this be a hire or AI?":** paste any job description → see which tasks AI can do, monthly saving vs salary, and a ready setup. One SEO/AEO page per job title ("AI instead of hiring a telecaller", ~100 titles). Monetize with a paid setup pack (₹4,999–9,999 / $99–199 one-time) that configures prompts, templates and workflows on the tools the buyer already uses. Upfront payment counts in full.
2. **Outreach list:** companies with fresh posts for automatable roles (seen manually on job boards / LinkedIn, no scraping) = targeted leads. Cold email still needs 2–3 weeks of domain warmup, so this mostly helps after week 2.
**Odds:** P($15K+) ~5–7% · P(#1) ~1%. Below the Homebuyer Case Builder because it's broad, the buyer is a business (slower) and the paid tier competes with tools that already exist.

## 4. Recommendation
The job-market lens confirms the **"AI paralegal for consumers"** lane (RERA homebuyer case builder) as the best fit for reach + convert + market under our constraints: individuals with lakhs at stake, searching with intent, paying once. Keep "Hire or AI?" as a possible free SEO hook, not the main product.
