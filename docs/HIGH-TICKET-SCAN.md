## Best candidate: "Homebuyer Case Builder" (India)
A self-serve tool for homebuyers stuck with delayed/abandoned flats to **build and file their own RERA or Consumer Commission case**:
- Guided intake → **complaint draft** in the state RERA's format (Section 18 refund/interest), or a Consumer Commission complaint
- **Interest and compensation calculator** (interest from the date of each payment)
- **Document checklist** + exhibit index from uploaded agreement, receipts, demand letters, builder emails
- **State-by-state filing guide** for the online portals
- **Hearing prep pack**: likely builder arguments and replies, timeline, follow-ups
**Pricing:** ₹4,999 complaint pack · ₹9,999 complaint + hearing kit · **₹39,999–49,999 association plan** (one project's buyer group files together: the ~$400–600 ticket)
**Distribution:** high-intent Google search ads ("file RERA complaint", "builder delayed possession refund"), project-wise homebuyer WhatsApp/Telegram/Facebook groups and associations, r/indianrealestate, SEO pages per state + per notorious delayed project.
**Why it can sell fast:** money at stake is huge (₹50L–2Cr flats), buyers are angry and searching, and the tool is 3–5x cheaper than a lawyer.
**Risks:** not legal advice (self-representation is allowed, but word it as a self-help drafting tool with clear disclaimers); quality must be high (wrong claims hurt cases); state rules differ; trust for a new brand; buyers may still want a lawyer at hearings.
**Odds:** P($15K+) ~10% · P(#1) ~2%

## Runner-up: "Claim Fighter" (India health insurance → Insurance Ombudsman)
Self-filing kit: rejection letter analysis, grievance letter to the insurer, Bima Lokpal complaint, document checklist. ₹1,999–4,999. Big volume, but a lower ticket, and free helpers exist.
