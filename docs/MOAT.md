# Moat & why people buy Rocco (Nov 17, 2026, 11:30 IST)

## Honest starting point
In 29 days, code is not a moat. Amazon's own connectors + Claude can already analyze reports; competitors (DataDoe, Helium 10 MCP, Agent Central, PPC Prophet) can copy any rule or prompt in a week. So the moat has to be things that **grow with every user and every day**.

## The 4 moats we actually have
1. **Memory + proof (switching cost).** Every change Rocco proposes is logged with before-numbers, then judged on the next report ("worked, saved $412"). After 2 weeks a seller has a running record of what Rocco saved them. Leaving = losing that history. Lead with this, not with "AI analysis".
2. **Data network (benchmarks).** Anonymized percentiles across Rocco accounts (leak %, ACoS, TACoS, conversion by category). Each new account makes everyone's benchmarks better. Nobody else in the contest window can show "you leak more than 80% of supplement sellers."
3. **Audience (the daily content you proposed).** A daily, useful, Rocco-branded feed that sellers and freelancers follow. In a 29-day contest, an audience beats features.
4. **Personality + positioning.** "The teammate inside Claude/ChatGPT that works next to Amazon's connector," with a face, a voice and a price ladder. Easy to remember, easy to share.

## Your content idea: yes, with one twist
"Daily news on how Amazon ads/accounts are running" becomes a moat **only if part of it is data nobody else has.** Generic Amazon news exists (Marketplace Pulse, tool-company blogs, YouTubers).
**Rocco Daily (X + LinkedIn + site + email), 5 parts:**
1. **Leak Index:** today's anonymized numbers from Rocco accounts (avg wasted spend, CPC change, ACoS by category). Before enough users: public data + Praveen's own account experience, clearly labelled.
2. **What changed on Amazon:** 1–3 updates (fees, policy, ad features, BFCM dates), summarized in our words + source link (no copying articles).
3. **Leak of the day:** one anonymized roast/catch ("a pet brand paid $380 for 'free dog toys'").
4. **One-minute fix:** the exact thing to say to Rocco ("Rocco, check placements").
5. **CTA:** "See if this hit your account → free Account Check."
Each issue = 1 site page (SEO/AEO, llms.txt), 3–5 X posts, 1 LinkedIn post, 1 email to the list. Claude drafts daily, Praveen approves in 15 min.

## What makes people actually pay (conversion, in order)
1. **A real "aha" in under 3 minutes** with zero setup: Account Check / Ad Roast on an upload, showing $ leaking. Biggest risk: the connector path needs an approved Amazon Ads API app for the Ads connector, which most small sellers don't have → the no-setup upload lane must stay front and center.
2. **Proof it worked:** wins card ("Rocco saved me $X") from the memory loop; public Roast Wall.
3. **Urgency that's real:** price ladder (+$10 every 5 sales, live founder number) + BFCM dates.
4. **Low risk:** one-time $99+, 7-day refund, read-only by default, approval codes + undo.
5. **For agencies:** unlimited brands + white-label client reports = their own lead magnet ("Powered by Rocco").
